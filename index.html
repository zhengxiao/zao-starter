流动的抽象 · Flow

致敬 赵无极 · after Zao Wou-Ki

  • 手腕、手肘和鼻子都是画笔 · Your wrists, elbows and nose are brushes
  • 颜料会一直流动、旋转 · The paint keeps flowing and swirling
  • 双手举过肩膀 = 暖色，放下 = 冷色 · Hands above shoulders = warm colors, down = cool colors
  • 张开双臂，和弦变亮；动得快，有“刷”的声音 · Open your arms to brighten the chord; move fast for a brush sound
  • 手腕穿过画面中线，响一声铃 · A bell rings when a wrist crosses the middle
  • C 新画布 new canvas · S 保存 save · K 骨架 skeleton

退后到摄像头能看到你的上半身（最好是全身）· Step back until the camera sees your upper body (full body is best)
